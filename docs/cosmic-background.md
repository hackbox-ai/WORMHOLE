# WORMHOLE cosmic background

The login and setup pages retain a single centered form, a compact header logo, and a responsive 480px card. Deep navy glass, cyan highlights, and a violet accretion disk replace the former stone treatment. The supplied logo is adapted as transparent native SVG paths, with light and dark wordmarks and a compact emblem. No JPG or PNG is embedded in the SVG. The entry header contains only the logo; it has no theme toggle.

The default workspace uses coordinated navy/cyan colors and a subdued background under dense data surfaces. Alternate theme presets remain selectable. Background layers do not receive pointer events or keyboard focus. Reduced-motion preferences disable entry animation.

## Assets and source

- `web/public/brand/wormhole-logo.jpg`: owner-supplied original artwork.
- `web/public/brand/black-hole-universe.png`: 1672 × 941 PNG, generated with the built-in imagegen tool.
- `web/src/app/(auth)/auth.css`: entry layout and colors.
- `web/src/styles/wormhole-cosmos.css`: default workspace palette and background.

## Final image prompt

> Wide 16:9 login-page background for WORMHOLE. Cinematic black hole in deep space, gravitational lensing and a thin luminous accretion disk, restrained cyan and violet light matching the cyan robotic insect logo. Black/navy universe, sparse fine stars, subtle wisps of nebula. Black hole at the right third, dark quiet negative space in the center and left for a compact login form. Sophisticated, realistic, very dark. No text, logos, UI, planets, or border.
