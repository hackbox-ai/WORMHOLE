# Fingerprint management

## Management Rules

Fingerprint libraries are used to identify products and technologies. Search rules by source, product or keyword, view matching conditions and status, and add or maintain rules through the entrance provided on the page.

Response sample verification is used to check rule matching and does not replace real target testing.

## Task linkage

After the task obtains the entry response, page or related materials, the recognition results can supplement the product information for planning and PoC association. Obtaining the material first and then identifying it cannot guarantee a hit without a response.

Check the evidence, rule source, and identification status when viewing asset fingerprints. Lack of icons and other materials, pending processing or recognition failure should be judged according to the page status.

## Judgment boundary

Fingerprint hits indicate product clues, but do not mean that the vulnerability is established; misses do not mean that the target does not have related products. After automatic recognition is turned off, historical data can still be viewed.
