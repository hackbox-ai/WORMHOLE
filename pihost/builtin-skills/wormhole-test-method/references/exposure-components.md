# Exposure, component and asset boundaries

## Content
Source map, SCM/backup, configuration, error envelope, export fields and storage in real references; normal/random resource comparison body/hash/type excludes unified SPA.
Check the field publicity, object ownership and independent private content; the framework package name/version/intranet IP is different from the complete credentials.

## Credentials
Public key/anonymous public key/example/expiration is separated from the real secret. Only when the current task explicitly allows it, use low-risk identity/read-only canary and wrong credentials to confirm the validity and scope; do not use new keys to expand the scope and record desensitization.

## CVE/Variant
Accurate build/configuration/reachable entry/rollback patch, unique effect of minimum harmless PoC compared with normal/absent; banner hit does not mean unrepaired/RCE. The root cause searches adjacent entries and does not reuse unmatched version payloads.

## Deploy legacy and identity clues
When there is a lack of valid test identity, or the public entrance does not have account information, turn to the information left by the deployment and migration process. These traces are common in real environments and are worthy of verification one by one:
1. The Disallow of robots.txt is only a path clue and does not prove that the directory exists or allows access; for a small amount of access within the task scope, exclude unified error pages and SPA rollbacks, and then check the directory index or archive files.
2. Archive/migration directories (archive, migration, backup class naming) are often stratified by year or version; construct a hierarchy based on the year and version number visible on the site, and verify a small number of adjacent variants.
3. Stale entries in sitemap.xml point to historical resources that have been offline but are still accessible; entries with a large difference between lastmod and the current version are given priority.
4. Migration, version and TODO comments in front-end JS often reveal the archive location and time; comments in the decommissioned equipment/service list often point to the configuration archive location.
5. Data forms in the archive: account mapping, synchronized export of personnel or equipment, and initialization list. Be careful to differentiate between active and deactivated entries (status column, timestamp); providing only descriptions of named assets is as valuable as account listings.
After finding the material, transfer to the candidate derivation of [Authentication and Session] (${SKILL_DIR}/references/auth-session.md); the account file itself is not equal to the login identity, and ends with real login and protected resource access.

## External assets
OSINT/Certificate/DNS/JS new domains are only candidates and cannot be automatically tested. Leave the CNAME empty to check the DNS and supplier resource status, and do not rush to register if there is no scope permission. Dependence obfuscation only relies on your own registry/build, and prohibits installation induced by public malicious packages.
