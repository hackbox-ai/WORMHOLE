# Bundled fingerprint data

Imported without flattening matching predicates from yyhuni/xingrin, directory backend/fingerprints.
The original XingRin MIT license is retained in XINGRIN-LICENSE.txt.
Original source families: EHole, Goby, Wappalyzer, chainreactors/Fingers, FingerPrintHub, ARL.
Source repository: https://github.com/yyhuni/xingrin
Matching dependency: github.com/chainreactors/fingers v1.1.0 (including its wappalyzer component).
The Gatyusha offline adapter is maintained separately under pihost/fingerprint-engine.
Content hashes and original counts are documented in docs/fingerprint-integration-validation.md and the adaptation inventory.

The fingers v1.1.0 Go module does not include a root LICENSE file; its bundled Wappalyzer license is retained separately. Original data and notices are kept as received.
